learn-deepseek-harness

Rebuild a minimal DeepSeek Harness in stdlib Python, one mechanism at a time.

A build-it-yourself tutorial on deepseek-harness (dsh): the reader rebuilds a mini-dsh in zero-dependency stdlib Python, with the real system as the reference.
